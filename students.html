For students, ages 16–22

You’re more capable than your résumé can say. Prove it instead.

A Fellowship is a paid, scoped project for a real employer — real work, a real deliverable, real proof.

What a Fellowship is

A defined project — research, marketing, or AI implementation — for a company that needs it done. Paid, with scope and deliverable agreed up front. You work directly with the employer; we stand behind you with a network of support.

What you get

Payment. This is work, not exposure.

Proof. A completed project for a real company is the strongest signal there is.

A reference that means something. An employer who paid for your work and would do it again beats any recommendation letter.

Who should apply

You’re 16–22 and you’ve built, written, researched, or shipped something real — whether or not anyone told you to. No prestigious school, polished résumé, or connections required. Just evidence of what you can do.

How it works

  1. ApplyTell us about yourself and show us the best thing you’ve made. Fifteen minutes.
  2. Get vettedWe review your work and talk with you directly.
  3. Get matchedWe pair you with a paid project that fits your skills — San Francisco, Washington D.C., or remote.
  4. DeliverYou do the work with our support. The finished project becomes proof you carry everywhere after.
Do I need to be in college?
No. Fellows are 16–22 — in high school, in college, or in neither.
How much time does a Fellowship take?
Projects are scoped to fit alongside school — typically 5–10 hours a week for a few weeks during school, or full time during breaks.
What does it cost?
Nothing. You get paid for your work. Future Fellows earns a fee from the company, depending on the work done.

What happens next

  1. We read what you sent and come back to you by email, usually within a few days.
  2. If it looks like a fit, we set up a short call to scope the deliverable and timeline. Thirty minutes of your time.
  3. We match a vetted Fellow whose demonstrated work fits, and show you why. You decide whether to go ahead.

Questions in the meantime? Reach Rory directly at info@joinfellows.org.

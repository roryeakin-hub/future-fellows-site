For employers

Build your next-generation talent today.

Fellows get work done, provide leadership opportunities for new teams and managers, and add new skills to your organization. A good deal, not a good deed.

The pitch

There is a pool of 16–22 year-olds who can genuinely do the work — research briefs, competitive analyses, content execution, AI workflow builds — and who are invisible to every hiring channel you use. We find them, vet them, and match them to a project you need done. If the work isn’t good, you don’t come back; we know that, and it disciplines everything we do.

How an engagement works

  1. ScopeWe define the project with you: deliverable and timeline. Thirty minutes of your time.
  2. MatchWe select a vetted Fellow whose demonstrated work fits — and show you why. You make the decision to extend an offer or not.
  3. DeliverThe Fellow does the work with a clear check-in structure. Future Fellows stays engaged for quality and accountability.
  4. DecideKeep the deliverable, run another project, or recruit the Fellow directly. The pipeline is yours.

Types of Fellows

Builders. These young people have extensive experience with AI. They have built custom agents and can partner with you on workflow automation, prompt and agent design, and building new internal tools with AI. If you are just starting to explore AI, or are already on your way, an engagement with an AI-native Builder can accelerate your internal learning.

Researchers. Researchers are screened for strong analytics, creative problem solving, and experience using new tools like LLMs to enhance their analytical capabilities. Outputs are typically words and presentations — market maps, competitive analyses, literature reviews, diligence support, data synthesis — not technical products. Consider a Researcher to advance a new project, add capacity to a current team, or bring a new perspective to your organization.

Artists. Artists also make things, but with a different perspective, toolset, and output. They help design and redesign websites, presentations, and customer interactions. While Artist Fellows can be self-taught, we look for candidates who are up to date on the latest software tools across design and AI. Consider Artists for projects to test assumptions and present innovative alternatives for products, positioning, and branding. Outputs here include customer-facing products, content, and campaign execution — especially for products targeting young consumers.
